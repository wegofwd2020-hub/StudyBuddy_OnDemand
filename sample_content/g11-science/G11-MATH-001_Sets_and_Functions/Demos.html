9. Composition of functions

Mermaid flowchart

Worked Example 1 (tutorial § 4.1). $f(x) = 2x+3$, $g(x) = x^2 - 1$. The composition $g \circ f$ is read right-to-left: $f$ first, then $g$.

12. Projectile motion

HTML canvas + requestAnimationFrame

Worked Example 2 (tutorial § 5.3). $h(t) = -4.9t^2 + 14t + 2$. Maximum height $\approx 12$ m at $t \approx 1.43$ s; ground impact at $t \approx 2.71$ s.

t = 0.00 s · h = 2.00 m
